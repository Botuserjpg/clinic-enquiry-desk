What the safety floor does not cover

  • A clear phrase scan is not proof that an enquiry is safe.
  • Negated and look-alike phrases are over-triaged on purpose.
  • Clinical urgency that is not in the phrase list can still be missed.
  • No reply is sent by this demo. A human approves every draft.
  • Emergency wording can never be downgraded by tone, language, complaint or injection rules.
